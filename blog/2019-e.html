Leonardo da Vinci: Vitruvian Man

2019-10-14T18:00
This year marks the 500th anniversary of the death of Leonardo da Vinci.
This drawing of the human body, from the collection of the Gallerie dell'Accademia in Venice, is a representation of the ideal body scale of ancient Rome. The words "尺" and "尺骨" (ulna) are derived from the Chinese oracle bone script "尸" (corpse). Even today, the body remains the standard for measurement, whether in the East or the West.

As Rikyu taught us in "Shuhari": "Even if you master and break the rules, never forget the foundation." It is very important to understand how to determine the dimensions of my bowls.

Comparison

2019-10-03T22:00
Glue lacquer made from Chinese and Japanese lacquer.
For several years now, the quality of Chinese products has been depicted very negatively in the media.
I made dry lacquer bowls with both saps. All other aspects of the production conditions were identical.

Please come and see them at the exhibition. We look forward to seeing you.
Both Chinese and Japanese lacquer have their own unique characteristics.
In this exhibition, I would like to discuss the differences between the various species of plants and the tapping techniques that suit the trees. It is important to make good use of the characteristics of both.

Documents

2019-06-15T12:00
Records preserved in the history of Joboji-cho.
Letter on the management of lacquer trees (c. 1596-1623).
Kemanai (former territory of the Nanbu domain) is part of present-day Kazuno City, Akita Prefecture.

The Nanbu clan "Zassho" (1652-1653) lacquer tax report clearly records the place names of Kazuno, Ninohe-gun, and Joboji.
This is a lacquer tree variety that requires a peeling sickle, a tool that has survived since the feudal era. There are fewer of them now.

Authenticity is an essential concept in World Heritage preservation.
